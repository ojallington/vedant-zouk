What is Brazilian Zouk

Two people, one piece of music, no script.

Brazilian Zouk is a partner dance from Brazil. It grew out of Lambada, slowed down and softened, and kept the thing that made Lambada special: two people moving as one to the music.

Nothing is choreographed. The lead suggests, the follow answers, and the dance is whatever the two of you make of it in that moment. The movement is flowing and wave-like — long steps, soft turns and, further along, the head movement Zouk is known for.

You don't need a partner and you don't need any dance experience. Level 1 assumes you have never danced before. Come alone or bring someone — either works.

Led through connection, not choreography.

    Questions

    Before your first class.

    Do I need a partner?

    No. Come alone or with someone — either works. You don't need to bring anyone to take part, and you'll be dancing from the first class.

    Do I need any experience?

    No. Level 1 assumes you have never danced before. If you already know the basic step and basic turns, Level 3 is for you — and if you're unsure, write to us or start with a trial class at Level 1.

    What should I wear or bring?

    Comfortable clothes you can move in, and clean indoor shoes with smooth soles — socks work too. Bring water.

    Can I try one class first?

    Yes. The trial class is for either level, no commitment. If you like it, book a single class or a 4-week course afterwards.

    What if I miss a week of a course?

    Write to us. Missed weeks are handled case by case — we would rather you come back than drop out.

    Can I pay cash?

    Yes, at the door. Choose “cash at the door” when you book and we'll hold your place; you pay when you arrive.
