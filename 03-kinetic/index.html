01 — What is Brazilian Zouk

Led through connection, not choreography.

Brazilian Zouk is a partner dance from Brazil. It grew out of Lambada, slowed down and found its own music — today it is danced to everything from Brazilian pop to R&B, remixes and slow electronic tracks.

It is led, not memorised. One person leads, one follows, and the movement travels between you: through the hands, the frame and, later, the head. There is no routine to learn. You listen to your partner and the music, and the dance happens.

The result is smooth and wave-like — long, continuous movement that looks like a lot but starts from one simple basic step. You do not need a partner and you do not need any dance experience. Come alone or with a friend. Level 1 starts from zero.

06 — Good to know

Questions we get every week.

Do I need a partner?

No. Most people come on their own. If you do come with someone, the partner deal takes 15% off for both of you.

Do I need dance experience?

No. Level 1 assumes none — we start with the basic step and how to hold each other, and build from there. If you already dance the basic step and basic turns, Level 3 is for you.

What should I wear or bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, and clean indoor shoes with smooth soles — Zouk turns a lot, and grippy trainers make that hard. Bring water.

Can I try one class first?

Yes. Your first class is a trial at €12, either level, no commitment. If you like it, book a single class or a 4-week course afterwards.

What if I miss a week?

Tell us as early as you can. A single class is not tied to a date, so just come the following week. For a 4-week course we will talk to you about the fairest option.

Can I pay cash?

Yes, at the door. Choose “Pay cash at the door” when you book — we hold your place and you pay when you arrive.
