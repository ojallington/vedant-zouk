What is Brazilian Zouk

Led by connection, not choreography

Brazilian Zouk is a partner dance from Brazil. It grew out of Lambada in the 1990s, slowed down, and took on new music — from R&B and pop to the kind of songs you'd never expect to dance to.

Nothing is memorised. One person proposes, the other responds, and the movement travels through both of you: flowing, wave-like, a lot of it in the upper body and the head.

You don't need a partner and you don't need any dance experience. Level 1 starts from zero and we rotate partners in class, so come alone or bring a friend.

    Good to know

    Before your first class

    Do I need a partner?

    No. Most people come alone. We rotate partners during class, so you'll dance with everyone. If you do want to come as a pair, book together and you get the partner deal.

    Do I need any dance experience?

    No. Level 1 assumes none — we start with the basic step and how to connect with a partner. Level 3 is for dancers who are already comfortable with the basic step and basic turns.

    What should I wear or bring?

    Comfortable clothes you can move in, and clean indoor shoes with smooth soles so you can turn. Bring water.

    Can I try one class first?

    Yes. Book a trial class — either level, no commitment. If you like it, move on to a single class or the 4-week course.

    What if I miss a week?

    Send us a message. Life happens — we'll help you catch up on what you missed when you're back the following Thursday.

    Can I pay cash?

    Yes, at the door. Choose “Cash at the door” when you book — we'll email you to confirm your place, and you pay when you arrive.
