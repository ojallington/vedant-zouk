What is Brazilian Zouk

A partner dance from Brazil. It grew out of Lambada in the 1990s and slowed down, softened, and learned to breathe.

There is no choreography to memorise. The dance is led through connection — a small change in your frame, and your partner moves with it. What comes out is flowing, wave-like movement: long lines, turns, and the head movement Zouk is known for.

You do not need a partner. You do not need any dance experience. Level 1 assumes nothing, we rotate partners in class, and most people arrive alone.

Questions

Do I need a partner?
No. Most people come alone. We rotate partners during class, so you dance with everyone.
Do I need experience?
No. Level 1 assumes you have never danced. If you are comfortable with the basic step and basic turns, Level 3 is for you.
What should I wear or bring?
Comfortable clothes you can move in, and clean indoor shoes with smooth soles. Water is a good idea.
Can I try one class first?
Yes. A trial class is €12, either level, no commitment.
What if I miss a week?
Tell us before class and we will do our best to find a fair solution, for example a catch-up in a later week. Just message us.
Can I pay cash?
Yes, at the door. Choose “cash” when you book and we will hold your place.
