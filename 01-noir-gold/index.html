The dance

What is Brazilian Zouk?

Brazilian Zouk is a partner dance from Brazil. It grew out of Lambada and kept the best part: the flow. Danced to slower, richer music, it is known for its wave-like movement, long turns and the soft head movements you may have seen on video.

It is led through connection, not choreography. One person suggests, the other interprets, and the music does the rest. That is why it feels more like a conversation than a routine — and why no two dances are the same.

You do not need to bring a partner, and you do not need any dance experience. Level 1 assumes none. Come alone or with a friend — either way, you are welcome.

    Before your first class

    Good questions.

    Do I need a partner?

    No. Most people come alone. You will dance with different people during the class, which is also the fastest way to learn.

    Do I need any dance experience?

    No. Level 1 assumes none at all — we start with the basic step and build from there. If you already dance and are comfortable with basic turns, Level 3 is for you.

    What should I wear and bring?

    Comfortable clothes you can move in, and clean indoor shoes with smooth soles (no outdoor shoes on the dance floor). Bring water.

    Can I try one class first?

    Yes. The trial class is your first class at either level, with no commitment.

    What if I miss a week of my course?

    Write to us. A 4-week course is built as four consecutive Thursdays, but life happens — tell us before the class and we will find a fair solution together.

    Can I pay cash?

    Yes, at the door. Choose “Pay cash at the door” when you book and we will email you to confirm your place.
