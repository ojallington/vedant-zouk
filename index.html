Before you look — a few things to check

  • The prices are guesses. €12 trial, €18 single class, €30 for both levels, €65 for a four-week course, €95 for both levels over four weeks. They sit just under what Zoukseed charges on Wednesdays. Tell me the real numbers and they change everywhere at once.
  • Card payment is built and switched off. Stripe is wired in and ready — it just needs your account connected. Cash at the door works as a full option, not an afterthought, because that's how the class actually runs.
  • Levels 1 and 3, Thursdays at Tanzstudio Rebecca, White Room. 20:00 and 21:00. Correct me if the times are off.
  • Still needed from you: an email address for bookings, a couple of sentences about you and Miriam, and whether you want it in English, German, or both.
  • Logins are the honest version for now. Each person's bookings are remembered on their own phone. Real accounts that follow you between devices need a small backend — easy to add later, and it won't change the design you pick.
